AI Access Panel

A look under the hood of a concept micro app. On the surface, AI Access Panel is a single web page showing whether AI systems are allowed to read public posts on LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube, X, Instagram and Facebook. Underneath, it's a process: public sources are read, checked and recorded, then shown on the page and passed on to other systems, such as a content calendar. This diagram lays that process out part by part. The app isn't built yet.
